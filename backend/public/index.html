Get your free AI key

ApplyFlow writes with Groq's free AI, using a key that's yours. Getting one takes a minute:

  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign in. Continue with Google is the quickest.
  2. Click Create API Key, give it any name (say “ApplyFlow”) and click Submit.
  3. Copy the key. It starts with gsk_. Groq shows it only once, so paste it into ApplyFlow next.

Tell it about you

In Settings, fill in your name, phone and LinkedIn. ApplyFlow suggests them from your resume, so check and save.

Then the step that makes emails sound like you: About me.

  1. Click Copy the prompt.
  2. Paste it into the AI you use most: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It already knows you from your chats.
  3. Copy its answer, paste it into the About me box and press Save. In a minute it shows ✓ Ready.

You can also edit the Email skeleton, the wording every email keeps, if you like.

Connect Gmail

ApplyFlow is in testing with Google, so Google only lets approved testers connect. Two quick steps:

  1. Send your Gmail address to the person who shared ApplyFlow with you, and they'll add you as a tester.
  2. Then go to Dashboard → Settings → Connect Gmail. Google will say “Google hasn't verified this app”: that's expected for an app in testing. Click Continue, then tick Send email on your behalf.

ApplyFlow can only send emails you approve. It can't read your inbox, and it never sees your password. Google asks you to reconnect every 7 days while ApplyFlow is in testing.

Not connected yet? You can still generate emails and use Copy text to send them yourself.

Start applying

  1. Open (or refresh) your LinkedIn feed and scroll.
  2. When a hiring post appears, ApplyFlow shows “Hiring post detected” at the top right. Click it for a job summary.
  3. Click Generate application: best resume, match score and a written email in a few seconds.
  4. Edit anything you like, then Send → Confirm, or use the 🕒 button to send later.

Nothing is ever sent without your click. Everything is tracked in your Dashboard.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. ApplyFlow is free to use, the AI key from Groq is free, and Gmail is free. You're never asked for a card. The only limit is Groq's free speed limit, a couple of emails per minute, which is plenty for job hunting.

Do I need a credit card for the API key?

No. Groq's free key needs only a sign-in (Google works). If a page asks for a card, you're on a paid plan page by mistake. Go back to console.groq.com/keys.

Will it send emails without asking me?

Never. Every email needs your click on Send and then Confirm, or a time you picked with Send later. You can edit, reschedule or cancel anything before it goes.

What can it do with my Gmail?

Only send emails, and only the ones you approve. ApplyFlow asks Google for “send email” permission only. It can't read your inbox, and it never sees your password. You can disconnect any time in Settings or in your Google account.

Why does Google say “this app isn't verified”?

Google shows that for any app that's still in testing, before Google's formal review. Testers who were added by name can safely click Continue.

Why do I have to use Developer mode?

ApplyFlow isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, and Developer mode is Chrome's standard way to install an extension from a folder. It only affects extensions you load yourself.

Where is my data kept? Is it private?

Your resumes, settings and applications are stored privately for your copy of ApplyFlow only, under a random ID that only your browser knows. Nobody else using ApplyFlow can see them.

Will the emails sound robotic?

Not if you add About me: ApplyFlow uses what your own AI assistant knows about you, keeps your wording, and won't invent skills or experience. Read every email before sending; you can edit anything.

Which browsers and computers does it work on?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave, on Windows and Mac. It works on the LinkedIn website (linkedin.com), not the phone app.

Something isn't working. What should I try?

Most hiccups are fixed by refreshing the LinkedIn page, especially after ApplyFlow updates. “The AI is busy” means Groq's free speed limit: wait a minute and try again. “Gmail access expired” means it's been 7 days: click Connect Gmail again.
